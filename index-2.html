About Urban Craft

Twelve years of finishing what others start.

Our story

Started on one site. Grown on referrals.

Urban Craft began in 2014 as a two-man painting crew working residential jobs around Kurla. What kept clients calling back wasn't marketing — it was showing up on the day we said we would.

Today we run painting, false ceiling, waterproofing and full interior fit-out crews under one roof, so a client doesn't have to coordinate five vendors themselves.

We still price every job the same way we did in 2014: a site visit, an honest quantity take-off, and a rate that doesn't move once work starts.
